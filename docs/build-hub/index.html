    Changes

    Newest first. Each entry matches a saved version in the project folder's history.

    • 21 Nov 2026

      AI assisted care, built and tested

      Families now see a short daily update written from the caregiver's records, with the full log one tap away, and "Why this match" on every caregiver. Seniors get a large Speak to Uday button and a companion that talks back. Caregivers can speak their notes. Operations gets Tomorrow's risks, care flags, spoken requests and a weekly quality report. Appointments and reminders are added, with dates suggested from documents for the family to confirm. AI only summarises, flags and suggests, and is used only when a family turns on AI help. Still needed: Claude and Sarvam AI accounts, and voice testing with real seniors. Not live yet.

    • 17 Nov 2026

      Readiness sprint: safety, privacy and app store checks

      An independent review in four parts (security, care reliability, architecture, app stores) found that SOS and urgent check ins reached nobody and every message showed as sent. Fixed: alerts now reach family, emergency contacts and the Uday team, stay open until someone responds, and escalate. Added account deletion and data download, parent consent for family links, community block and report, and AI companion consent. 55 automated tests pass. Kept on a separate branch, not live. Still needed before store submission: the mobile app upgrade, privacy policy, and real icons. See the Readiness tab in Status.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Website ready for search engines

      Every public page now has its own title and description, a canonical link and a share image for WhatsApp and LinkedIn. Added sitemap.xml (including live care facilities), robots.txt, and structured data for the organisation, FAQ and facilities. Caregiver profiles, sign in and account pages are kept out of search. Test servers tell search engines to stay away.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Admin Settings: connect every outside service from one screen

      Operations now has a Settings area. Enter keys for MSG91, Twilio, Meta WhatsApp, Exotel, Razorpay, an Aadhaar check partner, video and Claude, then press Test connection. Keys are stored encrypted. Business name and GSTIN go on invoices, with CGST and SGST or IGST, marked DRAFT until a CA signs off. Partners for doctors, insurance and the tablet are uploaded from an Excel template. Families can switch on UPI AutoPay or card auto pay for weekly care. The phone app is configured for Apple Health and Health Connect; it needs a test build on a real phone. 40 automated tests pass.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Family web app, senior mode and family mode built out

      Families can invite relatives, upload prescriptions, see the caregiver on a live map, chat, download invoices, join Uday Plus and use coupons. Senior mode now runs fully in Hindi and Marathi, adds Talk to Uday companion chat, a tablet layout, health sync and interest buttons for insurance and the Uday tablet. Every row left Partial needs an outside account: SMS, WhatsApp, Exotel, Razorpay live keys and mandates, KYC, video, Claude API key, GSTIN, and a native speaker to check translations. 33 automated tests pass.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      All nine journey gaps built and the journey run again

      Live in care now runs with one start code, a daily close and one end code; a relief caregiver covers the weekly day off; monthly care is billed weekly with an Extend option; families can ask for a different caregiver mid booking; a caregiver who has not arrived 30 minutes after start triggers an alert; caregivers have checked photos and public reviews; families can ask for an intro call or a trial day; the senior sees who is coming. 29 end to end and 14 engine tests pass.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Full journey tested end to end

      A caregiver joined, was approved, and was hired by a family for 24 hour live in care for one month; all 30 days were completed and rated, on the real code. See the Journey tab for every screen and the steps still missing.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Senior mode restored from the first Uday builds

      Seniors now get the full experience from the original prototype: four ways to check in (okay, some help, medical help, lonely), a home screen with streak, SOS, family call buttons and thought of the day, medicines with a Taken button, three brain games, interest groups, events and people nearby with a namaste greeting, and safety help for cyber fraud (1930) and crime (112). Families see a seven day view. 27 end to end and 12 engine tests pass.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Care facilities, wearables, fairer ratings and home commitments

      Families can now browse care facilities, book visits, use the visit checklist from your guide and rate facilities after a completed visit. Facilities onboard themselves on a monthly plan (prices are assumptions). Readings from caregivers, family and 10 common wearables appear together. Caregiver ratings use five areas, averaged, and a rating is needed before rehiring the same caregiver. Families pick the care need from 18 options in 7 groups and confirm what they will provide at home. 22 end to end and 12 engine tests pass.

    • 16 Nov 2026

      Caregiver app is now a separate app, with a daily care checklist

      Two apps: Uday for families and seniors, and Uday Caregiver for caregivers, each with its own store listing. The caregiver app adds a welcome screen with your photo, an academy header, and the Uday daily care checklist: time stamped tasks for morning routine, health monitoring, mobility and environment. Tier 3 nurses record BP, pulse and temperature; pain, skin and appetite for everyone. Readings outside the usual range alert the family and Uday at once (limits to be confirmed by the clinical advisor). 18 end to end and 12 engine tests pass.
